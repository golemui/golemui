gui-menu

A navigation menu the app writes as a named <nav> with lists of buttons and links. A group is a button with aria-expanded and aria-controls that opens its list in place; groups nest to any depth. Every item stays in the Tab order, and Escape closes the group around the focus.

Horizontal, with dropdowns

A first-level group opens a dropdown: its button has a popovertarget, and its list is a [popover] in a gui-popover. Groups inside open in place. A row that doesn't fit scrolls, with a shadow at each edge.
