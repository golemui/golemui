gui-menu

A menu of actions opened from a button (the APG menu button pattern). Click the button, or focus it and press Enter, Space, ArrowDown (first item) or ArrowUp (last item). In the menu, the arrows move and wrap, Home and End jump, and typing a letter moves to the next item that starts with it. Picking an item fires gui-action, shown in the corner.
