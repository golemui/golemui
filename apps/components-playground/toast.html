gui-toaster

toast() shows a short message in the page's gui-toaster. A status message is announced when the screen reader is idle, an error at once. A toast closes after 5 seconds, paused while the pointer is over the toasts, the focus is in them or the tab is hidden; errors and toasts with an action stay until closed. Escape in a toast closes it. gui-dismiss shows in the corner.

Variants

A title and an action

Queue and updates

Three show at a time; the rest wait. A toast with the id of a showing one replaces it.

Placement

In a modal dialog

While a gui-dialog modal is open, the toasts move inside it, where they stay clickable, and back when it closes.

Publish

Toasts sent from here show above the dialog, and their buttons work.
