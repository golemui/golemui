gui-tooltip

Rest the pointer on a button, or reach it with Tab: its tooltip opens after half a second. Escape closes it, and the pointer can move onto it without it closing (WCAG 1.4.13). It never opens for touch. Each tooltip joins its button's aria-describedby, so a screen reader reads it as the button's description.

On an unavailable control

A disabled button gets no pointer or focus events, so it can't show a tooltip. Mark it aria-disabled="true" instead: it stays focusable and explains why.
