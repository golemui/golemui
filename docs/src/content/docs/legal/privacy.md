### _Last updated: 31 May 2026_

GolemUI ("we", "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This website
(`golemui.com`) is the documentation and marketing site for the open-source
GolemUI form engine. This policy explains what limited data the site processes.

## Data controller

The data controller is **GolemUI S.L.**, registered in Spain. For any
privacy-related question or to exercise your rights, contact us at
**privacy@golemui.com**.

## What we collect

This is a static website. **We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking
cookies, and we do not build profiles of visitors.**

The only data stored is kept locally in your own browser to make the site work
and is never sent to us:

- **`starlight-theme`** (local storage) — remembers your light/dark theme
  preference.
- **`sl-sidebar-state`** (session storage) — remembers whether the documentation
  sidebar is expanded or collapsed.

These are strictly necessary, functional storage items. Under the EU ePrivacy
rules and GDPR they do not require consent, and you can clear them at any time
from your browser settings.

## Server logs

Like most websites, our hosting provider and CDN may automatically record
standard technical request data (such as IP address, browser type, and the page
requested) for security and to keep the service running. These logs are not used
to identify you and are retained only for as long as needed for those purposes.

## Third-party services

Pages may load resources from third parties, each governed by its own privacy
policy:

- **GitHub** — source code, changelog, and issue links.
- **npm** — package pages and version badges.
- **CDNs** (e.g. icon and font providers) used to deliver static assets.

We do not share any personal data with these services beyond what your browser
sends when requesting a resource.

## Your rights

If you are in the EU/EEA, the GDPR gives you the right to access, rectify, erase,
restrict, or object to the processing of your personal data, and to data
portability. Because we do not collect personal data through this site, in
practice there is usually nothing for us to act on — but you can always reach us
at **privacy@golemui.com**, and you have the right to lodge a complaint with the
Spanish Data Protection Agency (AEPD).

## Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected
by the "Last updated" date above.
